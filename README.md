## 🌟 Overview & Key Systems

### 1. 🏛️ 3D Picture Frame Dynasty Gallery & Imperial Court
- **3D Parallax Tilt Frames**: Interactive gilded baroque picture frames that tilt and separate in 3D depth based on mouse movement.
- **"✨ Enter Picture Frame" Immersion**: Zoom inside any frame into the **Imperial Court Room** of the Sovereign:
  - **Chandragupta Maurya & Acharya Chanakya** (Mauryan Empire, 322–185 BCE)
  - **Emperor Kanishka I** (Kushan Empire, 30–375 CE)
  - **Samudragupta & Chandragupta Vikramaditya** (Gupta Golden Age, 320–550 CE)
  - **Emperor Harshavardhana** (Vardhana Empire, 606–647 CE)
  - **Rajaraja Chola I & Rajendra Chola I** (Imperial Chola Thalassocracy, 848–1279 CE)
  - **Sri Krishnadevaraya** (Vijayanagara Empire, 1336–1646 CE)
  - **Akbar the Great, Shah Jahan & Aurangzeb** (Mughal Empire, 1526–1857 CE)
  - **Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj & Sambhaji** (Maratha Empire / Swarajya, 1674–1818 CE)
  - **Sher-e-Punjab Maharaja Ranjit Singh** (Sikh Empire, 1799–1849 CE)
  - **East India Company & British Raj** (1757–1947 CE)
  - **Sovereign Republic of India** (1947–2026 CE)
- **Lineage & Royal Family**: Detailed profiles for Queen/Empress, Crown Princes, Sons, and Grandchildren.
- **Waving 3D Silk Standards & Flags**: High-fidelity animated heraldic banners for every sovereign.

---

### 2. 🗺️ Subcontinent Territorial Conquest & Annexation Engine
- **Full Geographic Vector Landscape**: Spans 60°E to 98°E and 6°N to 38°N (Indus to Brahmaputra, Hindu Kush to Indian Ocean, Sri Lanka, and Malacca Straits).
- **Sacred River Arteries & Citadels**: Indus, Ganga, Yamuna, Brahmaputra, Narmada, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri, and 18 historical capitals/fortresses.
- **Chronological Timeline Scrubber (-322 BCE to 2026 CE)**:
  - Automated playback mode (`▶️ Play Chronicles`).
  - Real-time dynamic territorial polygon rendering showing exact borders for each epoch.
  - Side Annexation & Loss Ledger displaying *Territory Gained*, *Territory Lost*, *Decisive Battles*, and *Treaty Terms*.

---

### 3. 🧭 GTA V Style Radar / Mini-Map HUD
- Circular translucent HUD anchored on the bottom-right of the screen.
- Real-time 360° radar sweep cone and compass ring with North indicator (`N`).
- Focal target blip tracking active imperial capitals with live coordinates (`GPS: 25.61° N, 85.14° E | 322 BCE`).
- Adapted GTA V status bars:
  - 🟢 **Imperial Stability / Dharma**
  - 🔵 **Army Strength (Infantry & War Elephants)**
  - 🟡 **Imperial Treasury (Gold Coinage)**
- Click anywhere on the radar to center or expand the tactical map.

---

### 4. ⚔️ 3D Imperial Armory (Pure WebGL 3D Engine)
Runs 100% offline with zero external dependencies, featuring real-time lighting, metallic PBR reflections, and procedural Damascus (Wootz) steel textures:
1. **Khanda**: Double-edged straight heavy broadsword with reinforcing back spine, Hindu basket hilt, saucer disc pommel, and finger spike.
2. **Talwar**: Curved Wootz cavalry sabre with disc pommel, crossguard quillons, and sunburst finial.
3. **Chakram**: Aerodynamic razor-edged war discus with spinning trajectory.
4. **Bhala / Vel**: Imperial thrusting lance with leaf spearhead, bamboo shaft, and brass counterweight shoe.
5. **Zamburak & Topkhana Siege Cannon**: Bronze cannon barrel with flared ornamental muzzle, trunnions, and teak carriage with spoked wheels.
6. **Toradar**: Long Indian matchlock musket with spiral Damascus barrel, serpentine match hammer, and bone/ivory inlaid wooden stock.
7. **Katar**: Armor-piercing push dagger with parallel H-frame handlebars and reinforced diamond-section point.
8. **Urumi**: Multi-strand flexible steel whip-sword with spinning ribbon curves.

**Armory Controls**:
- 360° Drag Rotation & Scroll Zoom.
- **"⚡ Test Weapon Strike"**: Cannon recoil with flash/smoke particles, sword slashes with light gleam sweeps.
- **Lighting Presets**: *Royal Golden Hall*, *Blacksmith Forge*, or *Moonlit Battlefield*.
- **Tactical Stat Meters**: Reach, Kinetic Impact, and Armor Penetration.

### 5. 📜 Special Focus: East India Company Expansion (1600 – 1947)
An 8-stage interactive documentary timeline detailing the corporate takeover and national liberation:
1. **1600–1750**: Factory Fortresses (Surat, Fort St. George, Bombay, Fort William).
2. **1757–1765**: Conquest of Bengal (Plassey, Buxar, Diwani rights in Treaty of Allahabad).
3. **1767–1799**: Subjugation of Mysore & Subsidiary Alliance (Tipu Sultan, 1799 Seringapatam).
4. **1775–1818**: Destruction of Maratha Confederacy (Anglo-Maratha Wars).
5. **1839–1849**: Annexation of Sindh and the Sikh Empire.
6. **1848–1856**: Lord Dalhousie's Doctrine of Lapse (Jhansi, Satara, Nagpur, Awadh).
7. **1857–1858**: The Great Rebellion & Crown Direct Rule.
8. **1947**: Indian Independence & Sardar Patel's Integration of 565 Princely States.
